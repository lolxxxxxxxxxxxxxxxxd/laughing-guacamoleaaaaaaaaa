Working principles

Observe before you modify.

Prefer documented interfaces and tools that make behavior visible without changing another program's control flow.

01

Begin with supported instrumentation. Use a wrapper, dependency injection, debugger breakpoint, or documented tracing provider first.

02

Test in a process you control. Keep the target local, take a snapshot when relevant, and write down the exact scope.

03

Keep findings defensive. Learn how to reproduce, explain, and fix a behavior; do not use these notes to evade protections or interfere with third-party software.
